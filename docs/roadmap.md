# Next steps and acceptance criteria

These are planned improvements, not completed capabilities or promised partnerships.

## Close the remaining browser checks

Perform the native file-picker backup restoration sequence in `submission/2026-11-19/README.md`. Test current Firefox, Safari and a physical touch device. Record actual versions, actions and results; keep unverified checks labelled until performed.

## Evaluate whether people understand the decision

Ask monitoring coordinators or supervised student groups to explain a recommendation, identify an unresolved case and recover from a correction. Record task completion, misunderstandings and confidence calibration. No user interviews or adoption are claimed yet.

## Replace the fictional forward model

Work with water-monitoring specialists on reference sites, consistent measurement methods, timing, transport, dilution, duplicates, blanks and safe access. Validate prediction bands with suitable known-outcome data before producing real-world recommendations.

## Stress-test outside the model

Expand controlled tests with unmodeled causes, changing conditions, missing branches and correlated errors. Measure cases that incorrectly narrow to one explanation as well as cases that abstain. Compare with an expert-designed plan under equal data, access and costs.

## Preserve the project’s focus

Prioritize explainable decisions, honest stopping and recoverable evidence. Extra sensors, maps or AI agents should be justified by measured user need. The goal is a trustworthy investigation workflow, not an unsupported source-detection claim.
