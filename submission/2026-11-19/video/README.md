# Completed English demo

`Trace-Xylem-demo.mp4` is the final 199.5-second (3:19.5) video: H.264, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, AAC narration, and selectable English captions. `Trace-English.srt` provides the captions separately.

The film uses real Chromium screen capture of the working application, explanatory cards, trimmed waits and retimed footage. The original capture was 1180 × 700. All demonstration data is synthetic. Narration uses ElevenLabs' Josh voice and AI assistance is credited on the end card.

`video-qa.json` records completed decode, frame inspection and transcription checks, including their limits. `edit-manifest.json` describes the edit in source-narration time. No human listening review or field validation is claimed. Raw recording intermediates and speech-model caches are not part of this source repository.
